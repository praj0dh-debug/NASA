# Curriculum Standards Alignment Document

## Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) & Computer Science (CSTA)

### 1. NGSS-5-PS2-1 (Motion and Stability: Forces and Interactions)
- **Standard:** Support an argument that the gravitational force exerted by Earth on objects is directed downward toward the center of the planet.
- **Mission:** *Rocket Launch and Orbital Insertion*
- **Application:** Cadets calculate Thrust-to-Weight Ratio (TWR). The simulation demonstrates that unless the vertical engine thrust exceeds total gravitational attraction ($TWR > 1.0$), the craft remains on the pad or crashes downward.

### 2. NGSS-3-5-ETS1-1 & ETS1-2 (Engineering Design)
- **Standard:** Define a simple design problem reflecting criteria for success and constraints on materials, time, or cost. Compare multiple solutions.
- **Missions:** *Rocket Builder*, *Space Station Life Support*, *Satellite Design*
- **Application:** Trade-offs between fuel mass, engine specific impulse (Isp), and payload weight; electrical grid power budgets in space station microgravity operations.

### 3. CSTA-1B-AP-10 (Computer Science Algorithms)
- **Standard:** Create programs with sequences and simple loops to express ideas or address a problem.
- **Mission:** *Mars Rover Autonomous Coding*
- **Application:** Visual block sequences (`FORWARD`, `TURN_LEFT`, `TURN_RIGHT`, `DRILL_SAMPLE`) executed sequentially by the autonomous rover to navigate hazardous terrain without real-time joystick delay.

### 4. NGSS-MS-PS2-2 & Planetary Defense
- **Standard:** Plan an investigation to provide evidence that the change in an object’s motion depends on the sum of the forces on the object and the mass of the object.
- **Mission:** *Planetary Defense DART Simulator*
- **Application:** Linear momentum transfer ($p = mv$) during kinetic impactor deflection of near-Earth asteroids compounded across multi-year orbital trajectories.
